# Sources and accounting boundaries

- Crop straw source workbooks: https://zenodo.org/records/21334405 (2022 city estimates; physical collection ratios already incorporated). Source min/mean/max are parameter scenarios, not confidence intervals. Keep original publisher citations and license; raw source workbook copies included with attribution.
- Biomass energy and resource workbooks: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30993439.v1 (2020 and projected 2050; future potentials are scenarios). 2020 forestry and manure data underpin current constraints; 2050 projections are retained as evidence, not guaranteed supply.
- City livestock methane: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.25118063.v2 (2020 historical baselines and categories). The cattle-only lower bound subtracts all manure methane from cattle/buffalo total, excludes grazing deployment, and retains the reference feedlot/dairy eligibility assumptions.
- Collection cost: Xu et al., 2024, iScience, supplementary Table S14, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11253528/ (2015 USD cost components, with explicit scenario scaling, distance and farm-gate assumptions; not a measured 2026 roadside cost curve).
- Used cooking oil: USDA FAS China Biofuels Annual 2025 (CH2025-0151), p. 27; 5.2 billion L collected vs estimated 11 billion L generated. Only 25% of the estimated uncollected volume is assumed accessible, at 0.9 kg/L; existing uses not reassigned. See source URL and archive hash in cn17_V3_recovery_manifest.csv.
- Wastewater inventory: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26085265.v2; 2,665 facilities, not complete nationwide throughput.
- Waste energy database: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30828656.v1; already committed waste is not incremental feedstock. No new sewage/food waste avoided-emissions or fuel credit is assigned.

`cn17_prepare.py` rebuilds province tables from the source XLSX files; `cn17_inventory.py` extracts facility and waste evidence. `cn17_evidence.py` explicitly defines additional availability fractions, dry matter, disjoint crop pools, forest shares, cost assumptions, and independent manure/Asparagopsis accounting. These are scenario choices, not measured 2050 availability. The baseline NPK and soil-carbon pooled balance is retained; no field-level nutrient or long-term soil validation is claimed. A 50 Mt dry manure compost reserve prevents double allocation in the incremental digestion screen. No new gas sale, fertilizer sale, or fossil-displacement credit. The incremental biogenic CH4 GWP100 is 27.2; inherited transport factors are unchanged. Raw source ownership and publisher license remain with the corresponding publishers; citation information accompanies the files. V3 download hashes and source URLs are retained for provenance, not proof of scientific validity.

The 7 exhaustive deterministic scenarios and 128 coarse/refined conditional draws retain the existing solver, and an expensive liquid import proxy flags infeasible domestic supply; all new runs chose zero imports and zero modeled SOC deficit. Separate agricultural program costs and avoided emissions are added to the transport account rather than jointly optimized. No future business-as-usual reference, hourly dispatch, build schedule or confidence probability is established. Original reference results are not silently replaced.
